# Changelog

What changed in each version of the app. The version number is the commit count, and it's shown in Settings, at
the bottom of More and on the sign-in screen. Versions that only changed the website, docs or deployment aren't
listed. The app's What's new screen (Settings) is built from this file: keep the `## <version> · <date>` headings
and one change per bullet.

## 48 · 2026-10-03
- Receiving: a "Received, no location" filter lists what has arrived but hasn't been put away yet.

## 47 · 2026-10-03
- Settings shows the version number and a What's new list of recent versions.
- After an update, a short note says what's new in the version you just got.

## 46 · 2026-10-03
- Receiving: put a part or bag away from its menu, by scanning a location label or searching for the location.
- Receiving: every line shows where it's stored.
- Receiving: searching for a bag number also lists what's inside it, so you can receive the parts one by one. Checking the bag still receives everything in it.

## 45 · 2026-10-03
- Labels: "Print location description under the code" is on by default.

## 44 · 2026-10-03
- Put away: "Search locations" is easier to find, and it's where a computer starts. The location card has a Change button.
- Location search (Put away and Move) offers to create a location only when you type a code, not a description.

## 43 · 2026-10-03
- Labels: search locations by code, description or type, and select just the ones shown.

## 41 · 2026-10-03
- New sign-up page with your name and the terms, including that photos are read by third-party AI services we don't control.
- Signing in emails a code only to registered addresses, and links to sign-up.
- When the terms change, you're asked to accept them again.

## 40 · 2026-10-03
- Locations: delete an empty location from the list, or select several and delete them at once.
- Adding several locations: the preview no longer cuts codes off, and bins with columns are named RB-1A rather than RB-01A.

## 39 · 2026-10-03
- Pick lists remember which parts you logged as consumed, so they can't be logged twice. Undo is right there.

## 38 · 2026-10-02
- Receiving: search a kit by part number or description, and filter by To check, Received or Problems.
- Receiving: "Mark rest received" is gone. A kit's received date is set when its last line is checked.

## 37 · 2026-10-02
- Receiving: add a photo of a part from its menu. Lines with a photo show a thumbnail.

## 36 · 2026-10-02
- Export list: its own page in More. The CSV now has received and short quantities, kit names and dates, location descriptions and photos, and it works offline.
- Receiving: record how many arrived ("3 of 5"). A short shipment is marked backordered, and what's left is counted from what arrived.

## 35 · 2026-10-02
- Part photos: take or pick a photo of any part or bag. Search results show thumbnails, and they work offline once seen.

## 34 · 2026-10-01
- Fixed: re-adding a location or kit code that was deleted earlier no longer fails (and no longer undoes a whole batch).

## 33 · 2026-10-01
- Locations: add a whole shelf at once from a prefix, rows and columns (S2-1A to S2-5D, "Shelf 2, 1A").

## 32 · 2026-09-29
- Reviewing an import: the full-size photo shows the part number, details and flag message of the line you're checking.

## 31 · 2026-09-29
- Reviewing an import: "Done · next flagged" and arrows that step through flagged lines only, with a "Flagged 3 of 7" counter.

## 30 · 2026-09-29
- Operators get an email when someone signs up.

## 29 · 2026-09-29
- On a computer, a sidebar with every section replaces the phone's tab bar.

## 28 · 2026-09-29
- On iPhone in Safari, a hint shows how to add the app to the Home Screen.

## 26 · 2026-09-29
- The version is a number instead of the build date and time.

## 1–25 · 2026-09-26
- First releases: photo import of packing lists, search, QR labels, put away, receiving, pick lists from plans pages, offline sync, accounts and warehouses, email sign-in, members and invites, and the AI usage limits.
