How it works

From crate to shelf to the part in your hand.

  1. Photograph the packing list

    Snap every page. The pages are read in the background, bag by bag and line by line. Lines that were hard to read are flagged so you can check them against the photo.

  2. Label your bins

    Set up a whole shelf at once (S2-1A to S2-5D), then print QR labels on standard Avery sheets, starting from any label on a half-used sheet.

  3. Put it away

    Scan a bin once, then tap bags and parts as they go in. A bag's location covers every part inside it.

  4. Pull by plans page

    Photograph an instruction page and get a pick list of every part it calls for, sorted by where it's stored.

Install it on your phone

Nothing to download from the App Store. NowStocking installs from the browser, then opens full-screen like any other app. Installing it also keeps the browser from clearing your offline copy.

  1. Open it

    Go to app.nowstocking.com on your phone and sign in with your email. On iPhone, use Safari.

  2. Add to Home Screen

    On iPhone, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. On Android, open Chrome's menu and tap Install app.

  3. Open it from the icon

    It keeps a full copy of your inventory on the phone, works with no signal, and updates itself.

Made for the shop floor

Big type, one-handed, and it doesn't care whether you have Wi-Fi.

Forgiving search

Type "470ad45" and find AN470AD4-5. Dashes, spaces and case don't matter. A part that shipped in two kits shows both locations.

Works offline

Everything is on your phone. Edits made with no signal upload when you're back online, and none are lost.

Scan to see what's inside

Every bin and shelf label is a QR code. Scan it in the app to jump straight to that bin. The phone's camera works too, and opens the bin in the browser.

Receiving checklist

Check a kit against its packing list. Search a bag to receive its parts one by one, or check the bag for all of them. Record a short shipment ("3 of 5 arrived"), and put each part away and photograph it as you go.

Consumed and left

Log what goes into the build and see what's left. Parts sold by weight, like rivets, are left alone.

Moves and splits

Move a bag and everything in it follows. Split 225 pop rivets across two bins and each keeps its count.

A photo of every part

Snap a part when it arrives or any time after. Search results show thumbnails, so you know what you're looking for.

Export to a spreadsheet

Every kit, bag and part as a CSV: shipped, received, short, consumed and left, with locations. Works offline.

Phone or computer

Big buttons on the phone in the shop. A sidebar layout on a laptop for setting up kits, locations and labels.

Use ours, or run your own

The app at app.nowstocking.com is the same open-source code. Sign in with your email, invite your build partner, and read about 30 photo pages free, up to 20 a month. Or deploy it to your own Cloudflare account, where your data never touches ours:

  • WorkersThe app and its API, on your own domain
  • Durable ObjectsThe inventory, in its own database for each warehouse
  • D1Users, accounts and who can open which warehouse
  • R2 + WorkflowsPhotos, and reading them in the background
  • AccessEmail sign-in. Add a build partner without touching code
  • Email SendingLabel PDFs sent straight to your inbox

Set it up in an afternoon

Fork the repo, create the database and bucket, put Access in front of your domain, and deploy. Photo reading uses the Claude API with your own key, so Anthropic bills you per page read (typically 10 to 30 cents a page).
