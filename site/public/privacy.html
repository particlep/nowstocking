Privacy Policy

Last updated October 2, 2026

The short version
  • We collect your email address, the inventory you enter, and the photos you upload. That's what the service needs to work.
  • There are no ads, no analytics, no tracking cookies, and we don't sell or share your data for marketing.
  • Photos you ask us to read are sent to Anthropic's Claude to be transcribed. Everything else stays with Cloudflare, which hosts the service.
  • You can export your data any time and delete your account yourself, in Settings.

Who we are

NowStocking ("we", "us") runs the hosted parts-inventory service at app.nowstocking.com and this website. Questions about privacy go to privacy@nowstocking.com.

NowStocking is also open-source software that people run on their own Cloudflare accounts. This policy covers only the service we run. If you use a copy someone else hosts, that person is responsible for your data there.

What we collect and why

DataWhy
Your email addressTo sign you in with a one-time code, to identify you to people in workshops you share, and to send invites and label PDFs you ask for.
Sign-in codes and a session cookieTo keep you signed in. Codes are stored only as one-way hashes and expire after 10 minutes. The session cookie (ns_session) is the only cookie the app sets. It's required for the service to work.
Inventory you enterKits, parts, locations, pick lists, quantities, notes, and a history of who changed what and when. This is the service.
Photos you uploadPacking-list and plans-page photos, and the text read from them, so you can review an import. Photos you add to parts, so you can recognise them later.
Usage and cost recordsHow many photo pages each workshop has read, and what reading them cost us, to apply the free allowance and limits. If you delete your account, the page counts are deleted. The cost records are kept, with no link to you or your workshop, so our monthly totals stay correct.
IP address and browser signalsTo limit sign-in requests and to run the Cloudflare Turnstile bot check on the sign-in form. We don't store them with your account. Cloudflare keeps standard request logs for a short time for security and debugging.

We don't use analytics or advertising tools, and we don't build profiles of you.

Who processes your data

  • Cloudflare hosts the service and stores all of the data above. It also delivers our emails and runs the Turnstile bot check. See Cloudflare's privacy policy and its Turnstile privacy addendum.
  • Anthropic receives a photo only when you start an import or a pick list from photos, so its Claude model can read it. Anthropic handles that data under its commercial terms. See Anthropic's privacy policy.

We don't sell your data or share it with anyone else, except where the law requires it.

Workshops you share

Everyone in a workshop can see its warehouses, parts, photos, and change history, including the email addresses of the people who made changes. If you invite someone, we email them the invite on your behalf.

How long we keep it

We keep your data until you delete it or delete your account. Photos stay until you delete the import, the photo or part, or your account. Deleted data may remain in Cloudflare's point-in-time recovery for up to 30 days before it's gone for good.

Your choices and rights

  • Export: Settings → Export CSV downloads every item in a warehouse.
  • Delete: Settings → Delete account removes your account. Workshops where you're the only member are erased with their warehouses and photos. In shared workshops you leave, and your email is removed from their change history, but the parts you added stay for the others. If you're the only owner of a workshop other people use, make someone else an owner in Members first.
  • Anything else: to get a copy of your data, correct it, or ask a question, email privacy@nowstocking.com. We'll respond within 30 days. Depending on where you live, you may have more rights under laws such as the GDPR or California's CCPA. We honor those requests too.

Security

Traffic is encrypted with HTTPS. Sign-in codes and session tokens are stored only as hashes. Each warehouse's data is stored separately, and every request is checked against your membership. No system is perfectly secure. If we learn of a breach that affects your data, we'll tell you.

Where your data is

Cloudflare runs the service on its global network, so your data may be processed outside your country. We're based in the United States.

Children

The service isn't meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top. If a change is significant, we'll tell you in the app or by email before it takes effect.
